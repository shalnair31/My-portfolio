About Me

Where human values meet corporate rigor

I began my journey as a qualified social worker — a graduate in Psychology (Hons) from Indraprastha College, Delhi University, and an MSW from the Tata Institute of Social Sciences, Mumbai — training rural communities across Gujarat and Rajasthan in participatory development.

Three decades later, I lead the Global Learning & Development division at HTC Global Services, designing leadership programs and learning cultures that enable thousands of professionals to work better, think bigger, and step into senior leadership roles. Along the way, I have built an HR and training function from the ground up in Moscow, coached executives at some of the world’s most admired companies, and earned national recognition as one of India’s top L&D leaders.

The thread running through all of it: people development is strategy, not a support function. I am also a certified storyteller, counselor, life-skills coach and a Heartfulness trainer.
